FORMAT — two-voice dialogue:
- Two speakers: "F" (female teacher who explains) and "M" (male learner who asks the questions the viewer is thinking, tries examples and makes typical mistakes that F corrects).
- EVERY line of the spoken text must start with "F:" or "M:" followed by the spoken text. One turn per line. No other lines.
- Keep turns natural and lively: F explains in depth; M's turns are shorter but meaningful (a real question, an attempt, a mistake, a reaction). Use M's mistakes as the "common mistake" moments.
